This Privacy Policy explains how FORBET ZAKŁADY BUKMACHERSKIE SP Z O O ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile application "forBET: Sports Events" (the "App"), available on Google Play.

1. Age restriction

The App is intended exclusively for users who are 18 years of age or older. The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly allow persons under 18 to use it.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal data from users of the App. The App does not require registration, does not request personal information, and does not access personal identifiers, contacts, location, photos, files, microphone, camera, or any other sensitive data on your device.

3. Data we share

Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose any user data to any third parties.

4. Data we store

We do not store any personal data on our servers or on your device beyond what is strictly necessary for the App's basic operation (such as application settings stored locally by the operating system). No data leaves your device.

5. Analytics and advertising

The App does not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.

6. Permissions

The App requests only the minimum permissions required for its operation, as declared in the Google Play listing. None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit personal data.

7. Security

Since no personal data is collected or stored by us, there is no personal data subject to a data breach on our side. We nevertheless follow industry-standard practices when developing the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is restricted to users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone under 18.

9. Your rights

As we do not collect or process personal data, requests relating to access, rectification, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal data are not applicable. If you nevertheless wish to contact us regarding privacy matters, please use the contact details below.
