Privacy
Policy

Effective date: 18 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the forBET mobile application ("forBET", "the App", "we", "us") handles user information. The App is published by FORBET ZAKŁADY BUKMACHERSKIE SP Z O O ("the Company"). The App is intended exclusively for adult users aged 18 or older.

Summary The forBET application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device data, usage data, or any other information from its users. Nothing leaves your device through this application.

No Data Collection

forBET is designed as a no-data application. We do not collect, gather, request, store, log, transmit, sell, or otherwise process any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number, postal address, identifiers).
  • Financial or payment information.
  • Authentication credentials (passwords, security questions, tokens).
  • Health, fitness, biometric, or medical information.
  • Precise or approximate location data.
  • Photos, videos, audio recordings, voice data, or any other media from your device.
  • Files and documents stored on your device.
  • Calendar entries, contacts, or messages.
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (such as AAID), or unique installation IDs.
  • App activity, in-app actions, search history, or user-generated content.
  • Web browsing history or app usage diagnostics outside of those provided by the operating system or store platform itself.
  • Crash logs or performance data sent to the developer.

No Data Sharing With Third Parties

Because the App does not collect any data, there is nothing to share. We do not transmit information to advertising networks, analytics providers, data brokers, affiliates, business partners, or any other third party. We do not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that would collect data on our behalf.

No Account, No Sign-Up

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details in order to use its functionality. There is no registration form, and we do not maintain a user database related to this application.

Permissions

The App may request only those permissions strictly necessary for its core functionality to operate on your device. Any such permissions are used locally on your device and are not used to collect, transmit, or share information with the Company or any third party. You can review and revoke permissions at any time through the settings of your Android device.

Children

forBET is rated for users aged 18 and over. The App is not directed at children and must not be used by minors. We do not knowingly process information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has used the App, please contact us so we can confirm that no data has been collected (as a structural feature of the application, none is).

Security

Since the App does not collect or store user data on remote servers, there is no user data held by the Company that could be exposed in the event of a security incident. Communication between your device and the operating system or Google Play services is governed by the security mechanisms of those platforms.

Your Rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability. Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is no information for us to access, correct, erase, transfer, or otherwise process on your behalf. You may still contact us at any time with questions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application or in applicable law. Any updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review the policy periodically.

Governing Law
