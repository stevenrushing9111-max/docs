Cookie
Policy

Effective date: 18 May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the forBET mobile application (the "App"), published by FORBET ZAKŁADY BUKMACHERSKIE SP Z O O ("the Company", "we", "us"), approaches the use of cookies and similar tracking technologies.

Summary The forBET application does not use cookies. It does not store or read any cookies, identifiers, or similar tracking technologies on your device for the purpose of tracking, analytics, advertising, or profiling.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites place on a user's device to remember information about that user. In the context of mobile applications, similar technologies such as local storage, software development kits (SDKs), advertising identifiers, and device fingerprinting can perform comparable functions. Throughout this Policy, the term "cookies" is used broadly to refer to all such technologies.

No Cookies Used in the App

forBET does not use cookies or any equivalent tracking technologies within the App. Specifically, the App does not:

  • Set or read first-party cookies.
  • Set or read third-party cookies.
  • Use persistent or session storage for tracking, analytics, or profiling purposes.
  • Use advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AAID) for advertising or measurement.
  • Integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that would set such identifiers.
  • Apply device fingerprinting techniques to identify users.
  • Use pixels, beacons, or similar tracking mechanisms.

No Tracking, No Profiling

Because the App does not use cookies or similar identifiers, we do not build user profiles, track behaviour across applications or websites, or perform cross-device tracking. We do not deliver targeted advertising and we do not measure advertising performance.

Local Device Storage

The App may use the standard local storage mechanisms of your Android device only where strictly necessary for the App's core technical functionality (for example, basic settings stored locally on your device). Any such data remains on your device, is not used for tracking purposes, and is not transmitted to the Company or to any third party. This data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear the App's storage through your device settings.

Third-Party Platforms

The App is distributed through the Google Play store. Google may use its own cookies and tracking technologies in connection with the operation of the store. The Company does not control these technologies. For more information, please refer to the relevant policies provided by Google.

Your Choices

Because the App does not place cookies on your device, there are no in-app cookie preferences to manage. You can still control device-level identifiers through your Android settings, for example by resetting or limiting the use of your advertising identifier.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or in applicable law. Any updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. If we ever introduce cookies or similar technologies in the future, we will update this Policy accordingly and obtain any consent required by applicable law.

Relationship With Other Policies

This Cookie Policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, which describe how the App handles user information and the conditions under which the App may be used.
