Cookie Policy

Application: forBET: Sports Events (com.forbet.android)
Last updated: 18 May 2026

1. About this policy

This Cookie Policy explains the use of cookies and similar technologies in connection with the mobile application "forBET: Sports Events" (the "App") provided by FORBET ZAKŁADY BUKMACHERSKIE SP Z O O.

2. What are cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files that may be placed on a user's device to store information. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, web beacons, and tracking pixels. Mobile applications generally do not use HTTP cookies in the same way websites do, but they may use comparable local storage mechanisms.

3. Cookies used by the App

The App itself does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, fingerprinting, web beacons, or tracking pixels. The App does not set, read, or store any cookies on your device for analytics, advertising, or profiling purposes.

4. Local storage used by the in-app chat (Crisp)

The App integrates the Crisp Chat SDK ("Crisp"), operated by Crisp IM SAS, to provide an in-app support chat. To operate correctly, Crisp may store the following information locally on your device:

  • a randomly generated chat session identifier, used to associate your messages with the same conversation;
  • technical state information required to keep the chat connection working (for example, timestamps and connection status).

This information is strictly necessary for the chat to function. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or cross-app tracking, and it is not shared with any third parties other than Crisp acting as our processor.

5. Your choices

Because the only local storage used by the App is the technical data required by the in-app chat, there is no separate cookie consent banner. If you do not wish this data to be stored on your device, simply do not initiate a conversation in the in-app chat. You can also clear the App's data from your device's system settings at any time, which will remove any locally stored chat state.

6. Third-party services

Other than the Crisp Chat SDK described above, the App does not include any third-party services, SDKs, or components that set or read cookies or similar identifiers on your device.

7. More information about Crisp

For more information on how Crisp handles data, please see Crisp's privacy policy at https://crisp.chat/en/privacy/.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
